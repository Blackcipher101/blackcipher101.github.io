Demo 02 · Planning

MPPI playground

256 noisy control sequences are rolled out over a 2.6 s horizon, 20 times a second. Each is scored for goal progress and clearance. The plan (coral) is their cost-weighted average. Lime rollouts are cheap, violet ones are expensive.

Demo 03 · Underwater

Lights out

A Kalman filter integrates a biased accelerometer and corrects it with velocity from the camera and/or a DVL. Black out the camera with and without the DVL and watch the estimate (coral) leave the true path (cream). A toy model of the idea behind uw-vio-dvl.

03 / Open source

Current
projects

Underwater perception and estimation, with results measured against ground truth. Negative results are published too.

uw-vio-dvl

Visual-inertial odometry with a tightly coupled Doppler velocity log.

3.88 m → 0.66 mATE through a 20 s camera blackout, with simulated DVL

uw-gpu-klt

CUDA KLT feature-tracking front end.

2.0–4.9×faster, no accuracy loss

uw-inspect-report

A detector plus a VLM draft the inspection report.

8/8true findings confirmed · precision 0.89

uw-calib-health

In-water stereo calibration monitor.

≥ 0.2°every change detected · 0 false alarms

uw-vslam-bench

Underwater visual SLAM benchmark, with the negative results published.

Benchmark+ negative results

rov-rl-mujoco

RL controller for an ROV in MuJoCo's fluid model, against a tuned PID.

0.048 mvs PID 0.131 m in a 0.4 m/s current (beyond training) · 100 % vs 90 % success

Q14 Dynamics Lab

Robot-dog simulator in the browser: hand-written multibody dynamics (CRBA/RNEA/ABA), trained trot over 40 dynamic rocks, experimental SPH water.

1 kHzphysics in a Web Worker · real time on flat ground

turtlebot_autonomy

ROS 2 Humble stack on TurtleBot3: exploration + SLAM, a custom C++ RRT* Nav2 planner plugin, Isaac Sim. Semantic navigation (Grounding DINO + CLIP) is implemented but not fully tested end to end.

RRT*custom Nav2 plugin in C++ · Docker image
