### Company and vehicle photos: none used

I looked for public, reusable photos of the robots and vehicles behind the roles listed on the site:

- **Minus Zero (zPod):** the official site (https://www.minuszero.ai/) has no press kit, and its Terms of Use (https://www.minuszero.ai/terms-of-use) forbid reproducing or publicly displaying site content without written permission. Launch coverage in the news uses the outlets' own copyrighted photos. No zPod photo is used.
- **Aeroarc:** https://aeroarc.in/media/ returned 404 (checked 2026-11-06). No press kit was found, and the work is defence-related. No photo is used.
- **Clutterbot / BYTES:** no press kit or reuse grant was found. No photo is used.

Original line-art icons and the illustrated ROV are used instead. If you get a press image with permission, add it to `assets/img/` and record it in this table.
